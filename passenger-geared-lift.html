BEFORE INSTALLATION

Site Requirements

SHAFT SIZE

Shaft Dimensions

Minimum shaft size of 1500×1600 mm for standard 8-person cabin. Standard dimensions for a geared system require slightly more overhead space for the machine room above the shaft.

MACHINE ROOM

Overhead Room Required

A machine room of approx. 8–12 sq m is required above the top floor. This houses the drive motor and control panel. Our team provides detailed machine room layout plans.

POWER

Three-Phase Supply

Three-phase 415V, 50Hz supply required in the machine room. VVVF drive controller reduces peak current and ensures smooth, energy-efficient motor operation.

CIVIL

Pit & Overhead

Pit depth of 1000–1200 mm and overhead clearance of 3800–4200 mm required. RCC shaft construction recommended for all commercial installations.

CAPACITY

Persons per Trip

Standard models carry 10 to 16 persons per trip (1000 KG to 1600 KG). Ideal for office buildings, apartments, hotels and commercial complexes up to 8 floors.

COMPLIANCE

Standards Met

Fully compliant with IS 14665, NBC 2016 and BIS standards. We handle all statutory approvals, chief inspector certifications and operational permits.

Frequently Asked Questions

A geared traction lift uses a worm gear reduction box connected to an AC induction motor to drive the traction sheave. It's a time-tested, cost-effective technology ideal for low to medium-rise buildings up to 8 floors with speeds up to 1.6 m/s.

Yes. Geared traction lifts are widely used in office complexes, hotels, residential apartments and commercial buildings up to 8 floors. They provide high reliability, smooth operation and cost-effective maintenance — perfect for moderate traffic buildings.

Geared lifts have a lower initial cost and use proven worm gear technology but require a machine room and consume more energy. Gearless lifts are faster, more efficient and MRL, but at a higher initial investment. We help you choose the right option based on building height and budget.

Our lifts are equipped with ARD (Automatic Rescue Device) which automatically moves the cabin to the nearest floor and opens the doors during a power cut — ensuring passengers are never trapped inside the elevator cabin.

Geared traction lifts require quarterly preventive maintenance — gearbox oil check, brake adjustment, wire rope inspection and safety device testing. Our AMC covers all scheduled visits by factory-trained technicians with genuine spare parts.
